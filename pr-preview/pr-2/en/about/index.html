Daniela and Martin Kos

Daniela and Martin Kos are artists who have worked with porcelain for decades. Martin is a trained potter and studied at the ceramics school in Bechyně, Daniela studied ceramics at UMPRUM in Prague. They founded their studio in 1995.

Blue and white porcelain

The studio started with blue and white porcelain, and people still know it for it. For several years Martin threw it and Daniela painted it; in recent years Martin makes all of it himself.

Every piece is made by hand, from throwing on the potter’s wheel to painting with cobalt.

What makes the porcelain

Porcelain is rarely thrown on the potter’s wheel, because it is demanding to work. Martin has worked it this way since the 1990s.

The blue and white pieces are decorated with cobalt under the glaze or in the glaze. The decoration is fired together with the porcelain at up to 1,380 °C, so it does not wear off. The tableware is dishwasher and microwave safe.

Daniela Kosová

Biography

Born in Bratislava. She studied ceramics at SUPŠ in Bratislava, then at UMPRUM in Prague, and trained in Andrej Rudavský’s studio and in the porcelain factories in Dubí and Duchcov.

She decorates porcelain under the glaze, in the glaze and over the glaze, including gilding. For several years she painted the blue and white porcelain; today she works mainly on her own art.

Martin Kos

Biography

Born in 1959 in Hradec Králové; both his parents were ceramicists. He apprenticed as a potter in Týniště nad Orlicí and graduated from the ceramics school in Bechyně, as did his parents and his grandfather.

He worked at ÚVD Keramo Praha and has been independent since 1987. He makes the blue and white porcelain and turns more and more to his own work.
