Martin Kos

Throws porcelain on the potter’s wheel.

Biography

  • Born in 1959 in Hradec Králové.
  • Both of his parents were ceramicists.
  • Apprenticed as a potter in Týniště nad Orlicí.
  • Graduated from the ceramics school in Bechyně, as did his parents and his grandfather.
  • Worked at ÚVD Keramo Praha, independent since 1987.
  • Learned to throw porcelain and has worked only in porcelain for over 25 years.
