Porcelain

  • The porcelain is thrown on the potter’s wheel, which is unusual for porcelain.
  • It is fired at up to 1,380 °C.
  • The blue and white pieces are decorated with cobalt under the glaze or in the glaze. The decoration is fired together with the porcelain, so it does not wear off.
  • Dishwasher and microwave safe.
