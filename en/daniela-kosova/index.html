Daniela Kosová

Paints porcelain with cobalt under the glaze and makes her own artwork.

Biography

  • Born in Bratislava.
  • Studied ceramics at SUPŠ in Bratislava, then at UMPRUM in Prague.
  • Trained in Andrej Rudavský’s studio and in the porcelain factories in Dubí and Duchcov.
  • Decorates under the glaze (fired at 1,390 °C), in the glaze (1,250 °C) and over the glaze, including gilding (800 °C).

Collections

  • Museum of Decorative Arts in Prague (UPM)
  • Aleš South Bohemian Gallery
  • Slovak Design Centre, Bratislava
  • museum in Darney, France
  • Porvoo, Finland
  • private collections
